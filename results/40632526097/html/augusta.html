New User

Thank you for your interest in employment with Augusta University. For your convenience, this applicant portal has been designed to allow you to easily access the complete list of available career opportunities using your desktop, laptop, or any mobile device. Within the portal you will be able to:


  • View all career opportunities
  • Narrow your search by using specific criteria to quickly locate jobs that interest you
  • Create a search agent that will notify you when positions that match your search criteria are posted

To submit your application, returning users will need to log in to your existing account. New users will need to create a new applicant account.


For technical support or assistance completing a job application, please contact the USG Service Desk at (877) 251-2644 or support@usg.edu.


For specific questions about positions at Augusta University, please contact the Augusta University Office of Human Resources at (706) 721-9365 and/or HRTalentAcquisition@augusta.edu.


Due to an Oracle Known Issue, Mac OS users are advised to browse and apply for jobs using a web browser other than Safari.


The Jeanne Clery Campus Safety Act and the Higher Education Act (HEA) fire safety provisions require institutions of higher education to publish an Annual Security Report and Annual Fire Safety Report. These reports include crime and fire statistics for the three most recently completed calendar years, institutional policies and procedures for reporting crimes, and current policies concerning the security of, and access to, campus facilities and residences, as well as security considerations in the maintenance of campus facilities. The reports are available online at: https://www.augusta.edu/police/cleryandcrime/clery.php. If you would like to retain a copy of the report, you may print it from your browser or request a paper copy by emailing the institution at compliance@augusta.edu.
