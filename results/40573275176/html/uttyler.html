Advancing Healthcare for East Texas

We don’t just do — we also think about the effects of what we achieve and how we serve our region. Through a multipart effort to advance healthcare across East Texas, we graduate medical, nursing, pharmacy and allied health professionals ready to improve patient outcomes. Our National Institutes of Health-funded research not only leads to transformative discoveries but also sets the stage for outreach efforts that connect underserved communities with life-saving care.
