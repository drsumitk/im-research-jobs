Come grow with us!

We are growing and on a quest to provide intricate specialty care, innovative technologies and clinical trials not available elsewhere at the new UT Health San Antonio Multispecialty and Research Hospital. This $430 million patient-centered hospital is the first of its kind South Texas with a game-changing shift to proactive, personalized medicine.

UT Health at Kyle Seale Parkway is a five-story, over 100,000-square-foot ambulatory surgery center offering primary care, multiple specialists and in-house pathology and radiology facilities.

The Kate Marmion School of Public Health welcomed its first class in fall 2024. The research-intensive, community-centric school will focus on improved health outcomes and reduced disease complications and mortality in South Texas.

Forbes 2026 Best Employer for Company Culture 

UT San Antonio’s dedication to excellence in higher education is reflected in its workforce. The university has been recognized by Forbes as one of America’s Best Employers for Company Culture in 2026. This recognition places the university nationally among the top organizations demonstrating excellence in workplace, built on built on collaboration, respect and purpose.

Forbes 2026 America’s Best Employers for Healthcare Professionals 

UT Health San Antonio has been named to the Forbes list of America’s Best Employers for Healthcare Professionals 2026 based on independent surveys over the past three years, earning a place among just 250 healthcare organizations nationwide recognized for creating strong workplace environments for healthcare professionals.

UT San Antonio Health Science Center is the academic health center of The University of Texas at San Antonio. It includes schools of medicine, dentistry, nursing, health professions, graduate biomedical sciences and public health, and a clinical enterprise called UT Health San Antonio, which conducts 2.5 million patient visits yearly.
