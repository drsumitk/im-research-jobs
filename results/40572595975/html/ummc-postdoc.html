Overview

Our goal at UMMC is to provide a supportive environment and high-quality training for postdoctoral fellows. As part of our commitment to excellence in education and research, the Office for Postdoctoral Studies strives to serve as the dedicated resource for postdoctoral fellows and applicants, faculty mentors and department administrators.

If you are interested in a postdoctoral fellowship position at the University of Mississippi Medical Center, Apply Here

Advisory Committee

Members of the UMMC Postdoctoral Advisory Committee. Back row: Douglas McLaurin, Anna Funderburg, Sally Huskinson (Faculty Representative), Lais Berro (Director of Postdoctoral Studies), Nathan Campbell (Chair). Front row: Gertrude Arthur (Vice Chair), Manar Eissa, Megumi Mills (Secretary), Andrew Milner, Jordan Mallette

The Office of Postdoctoral Studies is supported by an Advisory Committee that provides and generates innovative ideas to enrich the postdoctoral training experience at UMMC. The members assist the Associate Dean in organizing seminars, Research Day, and workshops for the career development of postdoctoral fellows at UMMC.
