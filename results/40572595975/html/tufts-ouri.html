Opportunities at Tufts

The Osterweil lab is recruiting a motivated individual to fill a postdoctoral position in molecular neuroscience. You will be joining the exciting, fast-paced environment at Boston Children’s Hospital/ Harvard Medical School investigating cell type-specific mRNA translation in neural function and dysfunction (www.osterlab.org).

Our group has a number of hypothesis-driven projects that are aimed at identifying critical mechanisms in neural plasticity, and determining how these processes go awry in neurodevelopmental disorders such as fragile X syndrome. We are using molecular approaches including TRAP-seq, scRNA-seq, and spatial transcriptomics, and combining these with electrophysiological and behavioral assays to answer research questions and test potential therapeutic approaches that arise from this work. We are also employing new models to determine the conservation of synaptic mechanisms between mouse and human.

Candidates must have a PhD in neuroscience, cell biology, or a related field, and show evidence of good productivity in the form of publications. Experience with either molecular neuroscience and mouse brain surgical techniques, or RNA-seq and bioinformatics, is greatly preferred.

Please send a CV, cover letter with statement of interest, and 2-3 letters of references to:
emily.osterweil@childrens.harvard.edu. Applications will be accepted until a suitable candidate is found.

Boston Children’s Hospital is an Equal Opportunity / Affirmative Action Employer. Qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to their race, color, religion, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, protected veteran status or disability.

Boston Children’s Hospital requires all employees to be vaccinated against COVID-19 and Flu, (unless you are eligible for a medical or religious exemption).

Closing Date: N/A (until appropriate applicant is found)

And Beyond

We are hiring recent graduates and postdocs interested in applying their scientific knowledge, training, and project management skills to a career in pharmaceutical market research. While an advanced degree is required, ideally in the biologic sciences, experience in market research is not; we provide on-the-job training, extensive experiential learning opportunities, and formal courses as needed. If you’re looking for a way to apply your education and training to real world business problems related to the development of novel therapeutics for rare diseases, please consider applying.

About REACH. We are a startup market research company aiming to help the world’s life sciences professionals master any rare disease market, quickly. Our work is a combination of research, data analytics, and storytelling. We are looking to expand our team to help build on and accelerate our early momentum. Our clients include pharmaceutical companies, life sciences consulting firms, and academic institutions.

About the Role. REACH is currently looking for an upbeat and enthusiastic Analyst to generate disease market analyses and insights. A successful candidate will thrive in our fast-paced entrepreneurial environment and have the ability to autonomously conduct market research projects directly or in collaboration with contractors and junior staff. This role offers the opportunity for rapid professional growth and the ability to be integral in shaping our company’s research capabilities and operations. We are looking for someone who wants to take a fresh, modern approach to conducting and delivering disease market insights and solutions to the life sciences industry. As the company grows, this role can easily transition into a leadership role and we are looking for someone with leadership potential.

Key responsibilities include:

  • Conduct primary and secondary market research for rare diseases that span all therapeutic areas.
  • Author market research studies for our product solutions.
  • Conduct custom market research projects.
  • Create sales and marketing content (e.g., blogs, video animations, case studies, white papers, infographics, LinkedIn campaign content) to support business development activities.
  • Participate in client meetings and conference speaking engagements.
  • Take on special projects as they arise. Example past projects include webpage re-design (ranging from content creation to the visual design), creating marketing brochures, identifying and selecting a vendor for a specific REACH need, and designing prototypes for new products and testing with pharmaceutical professionals.

What we’re looking for. We care about the usual items (e.g., education, work experience, technical skills); these are the fundamentals that you’ll see below. We also care deeply about who you are as a person – your values, your soft skills, and your attitude towards working amidst constant change and challenge. If you are looking for a role that is a mix of core responsibilities (e.g., work aligned to your superpower skills) and new responsibilities that could change from month to month (sometimes week to week), then you just might be a fit for the REACH experience.

Qualifications

  • Advanced degree (MS, MBA, MPH, or PhD) in the life sciences with 0-2 years of pharmaceutical market research or consulting experience.
  • Must be authorized to work in the U.S.

Required Skills and Proficiencies

  • A wiz with Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel.
  • Strong data visualization skills. We are looking for not just the ability to create various charts and tables but the imagination and creativity to convey data, information, and insights in a visually compelling and easy-to-understand format.
  • Strong writing skills are a must. We value brevity in writing and the ability to convey ideas and insights in pithy and impactful statements.
  • Experience conducting primary market research (quantitative and qualitative) with physicians is required.

Benefits

  • Health coverage including medical and dental insurance
  • 401(k) plan with Company match
  • Unlimited paid time off (if you’re not taking time off, we will insist that you do)
  • Work from home with annual team offsite meetings
  • Flexible schedule with most working hours during business hours in eastern time

Location: Massachusetts (preferably Greater Boston area).

Apply. Submit your resume along with a one-page cover letter to Amanda at atheocles@reachmr.com.

View the REACH Market Research careers page for more information.

Funding opportunity from the ADCES Foundation and the Certification Board for Diabetes Care and Education (CBDCE), to support the career of emerging leaders in diabetes care and education. The research fellowship reinforces long-term goals by both organizations to build on existing research in diabetes care and enhance health outcomes for people with diabetes, prediabetes and other cardiometabolic conditions.

More information can be found on the ADCES Foundation website.

If you are an early career scientist who is interested in this opportunity, please reach out to the ADCES Research Fellowship Committee at grants@adces.org.
