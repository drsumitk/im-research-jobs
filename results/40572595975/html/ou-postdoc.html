Postdoctoral position - Clinical and Translational Immunology

Postdoctoral Research Fellow – Clinical and Translational Immunology

A postdoctoral position is available in the laboratory of Dr. Beatriz Hanaoka in the Department of Medicine, Division of Rheumatology, Allergy & Immunology, at the University of Oklahoma Harold Hamm Health Campus.

We are seeking a highly motivated individual to join our clinical and translational research program investigating the role of obesity and altered lipid metabolism in regulating immune cell function in rheumatoid arthritis. Our research focuses on human monocytes and T cells, combining clinical studies with molecular techniques, multiparameter flow cytometry, and functional cellular assays to better understand the mechanisms linking metabolism and inflammation.

The successful candidate should be able to design, perform, and troubleshoot experiments independently and contribute to ongoing research projects and manuscripts. Strong experience in multicolor flow cytometry and primary human cell culture is highly desirable. Experience with PBMC isolation, monocyte and T-cell culture, and functional immunological assays would be particularly beneficial. Candidates should have a PhD in immunology, cell biology, or a related field, along with strong communication and organizational skills.

To apply, please email a CV, a brief statement of research interests, and the names and contact information of three references to Dr. Beatriz Hanaoka at beatriz-hanaoka@ou.edu.

Postdoctoral Position - Cancer Biology, Obesity and Translational Oncology

Postdoctoral Research Opportunities

Cancer Biology, Obesity & Translational Oncology

Stephenson Cancer Center | University of Oklahoma
Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA

NIH/NCI R01-Funded Research Program (2026–2031)

 

About the Position

We invite applications from motivated and talented scientists for Postdoctoral Fellow positions in our NIH/NCI R01-funded research program at the Stephenson Cancer Center, University of Oklahoma.

Our research focuses on understanding how obesity, metabolic alterations, and molecular signaling contribute to gynecologic cancer development and therapeutic resistance, particularly in endometrial cancer. We combine mechanistic investigations with clinically relevant experimental models to develop innovative approaches for cancer prevention and treatment.

Current Research Directions

  • Cancer biology and Obesity: Metabolic dysregulation, adipose tissue signaling, and obesity-associated carcinogenesis.
  • Hormone resistance: Molecular mechanisms of progesterone resistance and strategies to restore therapeutic sensitivity.
  • Extracellular vesicles: Exosome-mediated communication and its contribution to cancer progression.
  • Preclinical models: Obesity-associated and transgenic animal models, organoids, xenografts, and patient-derived systems.
  • Translational therapeutics: Evaluation of novel therapeutic combinations and molecular targets for cancer prevention and treatment.

 

Candidate Profile

Applicants should hold a Ph.D. in Cancer Biology, Molecular Biology, Cell Biology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology, or a related biomedical discipline.

We particularly encourage applications from researchers with experience in obesity-cancer biology, cancer metabolism, or experimental oncology.

Candidates should demonstrate:

  • Strong expertise in molecular and cellular biology techniques.
  • Experience with animal studies, including tumor models, drug administration, and tissue analysis.
  • Ability to design experiments independently, interpret results, and prepare scientific manuscripts.
  • Research productivity, effective communication skills, and a collaborative approach.

Additional expertise in transgenic animal models, organoid/3D culture systems, extracellular vesicle (exosome) research, or bioinformatics will be an advantage.

 

Research Environment & Professional Growth

The position offers an opportunity to contribute to an established, federally funded translational research program within an NCI-Designated Cancer Center.

Postdoctoral fellows will have opportunities for independent scientific development, first-author publications, presentations at national conferences, interdisciplinary collaborations, and mentorship toward future academic or biomedical research careers.

 

Application Information

Applicants are invited to email the following materials:

  1. Updated CV, including publications
  2. Names and contact details of three references

To Vishal Chandra, Ph.D. (https://medicine.ouhsc.edu/directory/details/vishal-chandra-phd)

vishal-chandra@ou.edu

 

Position Type: Full-time | Location: Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

Postdoctoral Fellow Position - Dogra Lab

Postdoctoral Fellow Position — Dogra Lab

Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, University of Oklahoma College of Pharmacy, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

The Dogra Lab is seeking a highly motivated Postdoctoral Fellow to join a newly established research program focused on understanding how the tumor microenvironment, metabolic reprogramming, and cellular stress responses drive metastasis and therapeutic resistance in gynecologic cancers, with a major emphasis on ovarian cancer. For more details about our research, visit the Dogra Lab website: Dogra Lab

Research Areas:

  • Tumor–stromal metabolic reprogramming and therapeutic resistance
  • Aging and therapy-induced cellular senescence within the peritoneal metastatic niche
  • Development of 3D tumor–microenvironment models, patient-derived systems, and functional assays
  • Identification of therapeutic vulnerabilities using multi-omics, genetic perturbation, and phenotypic drug screening

The fellow will have the opportunity to develop an independent research project while contributing to collaborative studies spanning cancer biology, metabolism, aging biology, and translational therapeutics.

Qualifications:

Applicants should have:

  • A Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree in cancer biology, molecular/cellular biology, pharmacology, biochemistry, biomedical sciences, or a related discipline
  • Strong training in molecular and cellular biology techniques
  • Hands-on experience in mouse tumor models, animal handling, drug dosing, treatment studies, tumor monitoring, tissue collection, and downstream analysis.
  • Experience with mammalian cell culture and experimental cancer biology
  • Ability to independently design experiments, analyze data, troubleshoot, and communicate scientific findings
  • Strong organizational and scientific writing skills
  • A collaborative approach and enthusiasm for working in an interdisciplinary research environment

Experience in one or more of the following areas is desirable but not required: Cancer metabolism or metabolomics, 3D culture, spheroid, organoid, or co-culture models, Cellular senescence or aging biology, and Bioinformatics or analysis of multi-omics datasets

Training and Career Development:

The Dogra Lab is committed to providing a supportive environment for the development of independent scientists. The successful candidate will receive mentorship in experimental design, scientific communication, manuscript and grant writing, career planning, and development of an independent research niche. Fellows will also have opportunities to present their work at national and international scientific meetings and collaborate with investigators and shared research cores across the University of Oklahoma Health Campus and Stephenson Cancer Center.

How to Apply

Interested applicants should submit:

  • CV
  • Brief cover letter describing previous research experience, scientific interests, and career goals
  • Contact information for 3 references

Please send application materials to: samrita-dogra@ou.edu

Samrita Dogra, Ph.D., Assistant Professor, Department of Pharmaceutical Sciences, College of Pharmacy, University of Oklahoma Health Campus, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

Postdoctoral Position in the Eeda Lab - College of Pharmacy

Dr. Eeda’s Laboratory | College of Pharmacy, University of Oklahoma Health Campus   Location: Oklahoma City, OK     Position Type: Full-Time

Dr. Eeda’s Laboratory operates at the intersection of medicinal chemistry, radiopharmaceutical sciences, and molecular imaging. We design high-affinity, ligand-based small molecules and radiolabeled probes then deploy them as imaging beacons to visualize target engagement in living systems in real time. Our research spans targeted radionuclide therapy, SPECT/PET imaging agent development, and translational preclinical studies, with active programs in cardiac protection, retinal neurodegeneration, and oncology.

We are seeking a highly motivated Postdoctoral Research Fellow with strong expertise in cell biology, in vitro pharmacology, and preclinical therapy studies to join our growing team and lead the biological validation arms of our translational research programs.

Key Responsibilities

In Vitro Screening: Design and execute cell-based assays, phenotypic screenings, including 2D or 3D cell culture, organoids, viability assays, receptor binding/internalization assays, clonogenic survival, DNA damage assays, immunofluorescence, flow cytometry, Western blotting, or IHC

Disease Modeling: Establish and maintain robust cellular disease models, including handling primary cell lines, handling or developing iPSC-derived retinal organoids or RPE cells, and coordinating with collaborative clinical platforms.

In Vivo Animal Studies: Experience with in vivo tumor models, including xenograft, orthotopic, metastatic, or patient-derived models. Lead rodent handling, dosing (including IV delivery frameworks), surgical/injury animal models relevant to ischemia or ocular degeneration, and subsequent tissue harvesting/histology.

Data Analysis & Dissemination: Analyze complex datasets, prepare high-quality figures for publication, draft manuscripts, and present research at national and international conferences.

Qualifications

· PhD degree in cell biology or molecular biology.

· For best candidates, previous experience in any of the following areas is desired: cell culture and aseptic technique, and experience working with animals.

· Knowledge and/or experience in bone histology and mechanics studies will be viewed favorably.

· The candidates must have excellent project management, communication, and team work skills.

How to Apply

Send your CV, a brief cover letter describing your research interests and relevant experience, and contact information for three references to: venkateswararao-eeda@ou.edu Dr. Venkateswararao Eeda · Room 335 · OUHSC College of Pharmacy · Oklahoma City, OK

Two-Year Postdoctoral Position in Cancer Rehabilitation Research at OU Health Sciences / Stephenson Cancer Center

Laboratory of Elizabeth Hile

------------------------------------

The Cancer Rehabilitation Research Laboratory (CRRL) in the NCI-accredited Stephenson Cancer Center at the University of Oklahoma (OU) has an immediate opening for a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at an annual salary of $63,000 for up to 2 years.  The CRRL aims to optimize early detection and intervention for cancer-related neuromuscular toxicities impacting survivors’ movement and Quality of Life. Toward this aim, we collaborate with OU Norman engineers on related biotechnologies and artificial intelligence algorithms, and with OU Health clinics to recruit individuals with cancers, especially gynecologic, hematologic, hepatobiliary, and bladder.

Expectations: The postdoctoral fellow will expand their skills toward independently conducting, disseminating, and leading research.  They will accomplish these objectives through self-directed, active learning as they perform activities that include, but are not limited to: 

  • All daily research tasks required for active CRRL study protocols, including recruitment and eligibility screening of participants, the collection and management of human performance data and patient-reported outcomes, and the provision of study interventions.  
  • Preparation of developing study protocols and regulatory submissions for institutional research approvals. The fellow will also assist the PI in preparing intramural and extramural funding applications.
  • Analysis of existing laboratory data for publication and national presentation.  Authorship is earned, in an order commensurate with the level of independent contributions. 
  • Co-mentoring of laboratory trainees, including undergraduate and graduate students.  Didactic teaching opportunities (e.g., guest lectures) may also be available through the OU College of Allied Health.

Active attendance at research meetings is required, and the fellow must present their progress weekly. The submission of at least one first-authored manuscript and national abstract are expected in the first year of training.  Funding for a second year of training will be contingent upon satisfactory performance evaluations in Year 1.

 

Eligibility:  Requirements include the completion of a dissertation-based doctoral degree (PhD preferred), experience in the conduct and analysis of human participants’ research in an area related to rehabilitation, and a history of research dissemination in the form of presentations and publications.  A history of clinical practice is preferred; physical therapists eligible for licensure in the State of Oklahoma will receive the highest priority.   The applicant must have strong written and verbal English-language communication skills, the ability to safely engage with study participants of various age groups, cultural backgrounds, and physical function, and confidence to learn human performance technology.  Statistical assistance is available; however, we seek a motivated problem-solver with strong analytical and reasoning skills to clean, visualize, and interpret human performance data with minimal supervision from the PI. 

To Apply:  Send a cover letter (2-page maximum) detailing relevant research experience and interests, a curriculum vitae, and two letters of reference to Dr. Elizabeth Hile (Elizabeth-Hile@ouhsc.edu).

About Oklahoma City:  OKC is the anchor of a rapidly growing, multicultural metropolitan area with a population of 1.3 million, over 300 days of sunshine annually, very affordable cost of living, excellent schools, and a wide array of cultural amenities. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N-0cPNJPRLY; http://bricktownokc.com/  The University of Oklahoma is an EO/AA Institution http://www.ou.edu/eoo/ .
