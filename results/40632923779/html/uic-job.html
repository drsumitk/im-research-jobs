Position Summary
The research in the Adaikpoh Laboratory at the University of Illinois Chicago, Retzky College of Pharmacy (https://adaikpoh.lab.uic.edu/), explores the ecological relationships between plants and their bacteria to identify new antimicrobial natural products.

Duties & Responsibilities

  • Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:
    • Keep accurate records of the experiments suitable for writing manuscripts and grant proposals.
    • Present the results at scientific meetings.
    • Participate in weekly lab meetings, Journal Clubs, and seminars within the Department.
    • Design and conduct research and analyze results together with mentor, Dr. Barbara I. Adaikpoh.
    • Research activities may include bacterial genome analysis, transcriptomic studies, gene deletion, heterologous expression of bacterial gene clusters, metabolite analyses, and antibacterial and antifungal analysis.
    • Mentorship activities such as working with undergraduate and graduate students.
    • Collaborative activities such as working with labs within UIC and in other institutions.
    • Perform other related duties and participate in special projects as assigned.
    • Perform other related duties and participate in special projects as assigned.

    Minimum Qualifications

    • PhD degree in Molecular Biology, Pharmacognosy, Microbioloby, Chemistry, Biochemistry or a related discipline.
    • Publication record in microbial natural products research or a related field.


    Preferred Qualifications
    • Previous experience with techniques such as gene deletion, heterologous gene cluster expression, transcriptomics, and bacterial genome mining for natural product discovery. 
    • Previous experience with HPLC, mass spectrometry, and analytical techniques for metabolite isolation and elucidation.
    • Demonstrate technical expertise in molecular biology, microbiology, and/or genomics and transcriptomics as applied to natural products research.
